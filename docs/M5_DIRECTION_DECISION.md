# M5 direction decision

## Selected path

**PATH V2 — AUDIT IMPOSSIBILITY / MINIMAL-ASSUMPTION THEORY BECOMES PRIMARY**

## Decision

V-F is the primary model. Its exact finite-population result is clear: under unrestricted arbitrary row corruption and a clean-record audit oracle, any policy that leaves records unaudited has a positive worst-case interval for the clean recorded event fraction when `m>=1`. Full audit is required for a universal zero-width guarantee.

This is more coherent than immediately importing a classical V-P efficiency objective, but it is not yet a survival-specific positive contribution. The next mathematical question would be to identify the weakest transfer assumption under which audits inform unaudited censored records without pretending to reveal post-censoring event times.

## Why not PASS or FAIL

The impossibility theorem candidate is exact, falsifiable, and practically relevant as a guardrail. However, its core logic is not yet demonstrably survival-specific and existing contaminated-data, trusted-data, and validation-design literature are strong threats. A positive transfer-assumption theorem is not yet developed, so a PASS would be unjustified. The pivot itself is not a FAIL because the audit oracle distinction and impossibility boundary are coherent and testable.
