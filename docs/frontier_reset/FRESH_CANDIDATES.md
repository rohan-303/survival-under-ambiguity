# Fresh Frontier Candidates

All candidates below were screened for independence from archived censoring/OT/RMST/audit work and Candidates 1–3. They are questions, not claims.

| ID | Falsifiable question | Class | If solved, what changes? | Closest threats | Data/feasibility | Qualitative status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Can feedback-aware monitoring distinguish true untreated-risk degradation from outcome improvement caused by model-triggered treatment without randomized withholding? | THEORY/DATA | Monitoring estimand and holdout design | Performative prediction, causal monitoring | Deployment logs likely required | Low feasibility, high prior-art risk |
| Q2 | When labels used for generation `M2` are partly produced by `M1`, can provenance-aware evaluation identify recursive confirmation before retraining? | THEORY | Retraining gate and label audit | Label noise, automation bias, model collapse | Public data insufficient for real provenance | Medium novelty, high data risk |
| Q3 | Can a monitoring rule provide valid early warnings when labels are delayed and selectively observed because the deployed model changes follow-up? | THEORY | Label-delay monitoring policy | Drift detection, survival analysis | Public timestamps; model/action logs needed | Medium novelty, high assumptions |
| Q4 | Can uncertainty/selective-risk guarantees remain valid across clinical sites when calibration labels mature at different rates and subgroup sizes are unequal? | THEORY/BENCHMARK | Deployment eligibility and abstention | Conformal prediction, group fairness | MIMIC/eICU feasible; no model feedback needed | Medium novelty, medium feasibility |
| Q5 | Does foundation-model scaling improve discrimination while worsening uncertainty portability under code-system and acquisition shift? | BENCHMARK | Choose smaller/uncertainty-aware model | Portability paradox, external validation | MIMIC-CXR/CheXpert or EHR; inference GPU required | Low novelty unless precise uncertainty target |
| Q6 | When does learning-to-defer improve clinical utility after modeling clinician skill, workload, queueing, and disagreement rather than assuming a perfect human? | THEORY/BENCHMARK | Routing and staffing rules | Learning to defer, human-AI complementarity | Human logs scarce; synthetic-only penalty | Medium novelty, high data risk |
| Q7 | Can mixed label provenance be represented as a versioned estimand so that model evaluation remains valid when reference labels depend on prior AI? | THEORY/DATA | Audit and retraining eligibility | Label noise, circular evaluation | Requires provenance-rich deployment data | Medium novelty, high data risk |
| Q8 | Can temporal/code-system changes be separated from clinical concept drift using only versioned pipeline metadata and stable anchor cases? | DATA-CENTRIC | Reproducible monitoring and rollback | Dataset shift and MLOps monitoring | MIMIC versions plus controlled anchor set feasible | Medium novelty, high prior-art risk |
| Q9 | Can a patient-level unlearning audit detect when deletion preserves overall AUROC but changes calibration or subgroup safety? | BENCHMARK | Compliance audit beyond parameter deletion | Machine unlearning audits | Public models/data; moderate GPU | Medium novelty, medium feasibility |
| Q10 | Can a clinical NLP model’s uncertainty signal semantic drift before annotated labels arrive, while distinguishing terminology change from population change? | BENCHMARK | Pre-label NLP monitoring | NLP drift and uncertainty estimation | MIMIC notes access; annotation challenge | Medium novelty, medium data risk |
| Q11 | Can imaging uncertainty detect acquisition/protocol failure before labels arrive without simply flagging all rare cases? | THEORY/BENCHMARK | Label-free imaging safety monitor | OOD detection, calibration, domain generalization | CheXpert/MIMIC-CXR feasible | Medium novelty, medium compute |
| Q12 | Can a value-of-information policy select the next clinical measurement to reduce decision loss under a budget, rather than maximize prediction score? | THEORY/DATA | Test ordering and resource allocation | Active learning, decision-focused acquisition | MIMIC/eICU; action confounding | Medium novelty, high causal risk |
| Q13 | Can abstention thresholds be optimized for subgroup-specific clinical harm when access and follow-up after deferral differ? | DECISION | Safer deployment contracts | Fairness, selective prediction | Public data with explicit utility; real workflow absent | Low/medium novelty |
| Q14 | Can treatment-effect models be transported across sites when treatment availability and clinician adherence differ, with a decision guarantee rather than effect-estimate similarity? | THEORY | Policy transfer | Causal transportability | Multi-site data; strong assumptions | Medium novelty, high feasibility risk |
| Q15 | Does conditioning evaluation on label provenance predict clinically meaningful error differences better than treating clinician, rule-derived, report-derived, and prior-model labels as equivalent? | BENCHMARK/DATA | Label audit and error localization | Soft labels, label noise, circular evaluation | MIMIC-CXR, CheXpert, MIMIC-IV; provenance incomplete | Medium novelty, high provenance risk |
| Q16 | Can a limited expert-adjudication budget improve calibration and subgroup safety more efficiently than random, uncertainty, or disagreement sampling? | DATA-CENTRIC | Annotation allocation and reliability | Active learning, consensus labels | VinDr-CXR or MIMIC-CXR; prospective expert effort needed | Medium novelty, medium feasibility |
| Q17 | Can privacy-preserving multi-site auditing detect calibration and subgroup failures without allowing institutions to suppress adverse results? | DATA-CENTRIC/SYSTEMS | Accountable audit governance | Federated analytics, differential privacy | Requires genuine multi-site cooperation | Medium novelty, high data/governance risk |
| Q18 | Can explanations be evaluated by causal actionability rather than predictive attribution, under explicit treatment and physiology constraints? | THEORY | Safer recommendations | Causal explanations, SHAP/IG faithfulness | Requires treatment-response data or controlled study | Low/medium novelty, high identification risk |
