# M3 paper-core test

## Abstract A — weakest viable paper

> We characterize subgroup survival ambiguity under a global Huber contamination budget and show that the population diameter scales with inverse observable subgroup mass. We compare global, marginal-preserving, and groupwise contamination contracts.

**Assessment:** not sufficient as a standalone paper center. It is mathematically correct and potentially useful for audit reporting, but the one-bin formulas are elementary and threatened by general Huber geometry.

**No neural model/generator:** still true, but not yet scientifically interesting enough.

## Abstract B — strong theory paper

> We derive sharp finite-grid identified sets for subgroup survival curves and RMST under right censoring and exact Huber contamination. An audit-information hierarchy distinguishes global-only, marginal-preserving, group-capped, and known label-channel contracts. The theory gives a contract-level restoration frontier and a joint LP/dual characterization of how audit information changes horizon-specific and integrated survival ambiguity.

**Assessment:** potentially publishable if the theorem is sharp, the contract-level comparison is not a relabeling, and the literature kill test leaves a real gap.

**No neural model/generator:** yes. This abstract would remain scientifically interesting because it changes what survival analysts may certify or must report.

## Abstract C — strongest plausible paper

> We establish an operator-level modulus for subgroup survival functionals under exact contamination after a known censoring channel, prove an audit-restoration frontier, characterize RMST-specific joint attainability, and derive dual sensitivity rules for prioritizing finite data-quality audits. Targeted numerical and later empirical studies validate the population theory and expose overconfident subgroup survival conclusions.

**Assessment:** strong if every theorem and certificate survives adversarial review and prior-art comparison. The audit-prioritization claim must be contract-level, not a shadow-price over a fixed infeasible dataset.

**No neural model/generator:** yes. The scientific contribution is identification and decision stability, not generation.

## Gate consequence

Abstract B is the minimum acceptable theoretical core. Step 04 does not establish it. The project remains `CONDITIONAL` and should not move to synthetic generation or broad finite-sample inference until T-C/T-D/T-E are resolved.
