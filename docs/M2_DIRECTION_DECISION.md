# M2 Direction Decision

## Selected direction

**PATH R3 — GLOBAL-VS-GROUPWISE AUDIT INFORMATION BECOMES PRIMARY**

## Why

The trusted-label G-A formula is sharp but elementary: it follows directly from Huber neighborhood overlap after scaling the subgroup recorded law by its clean prevalence and follow-up. Centering the paper on T1/T2 risks producing a technically correct but unsurprising rescaling.

The stronger direction is to compare G-A, G-C, and G-D under explicit contamination contracts, then extend the comparison to label uncertainty (G-B) and multi-bin survival functionals. This makes the scientific question operational: what data-quality information is sufficient to prevent subgroup survival conclusions from becoming population-unidentified?

## Required pivot conditions

- Preserve T1/T2 as lemmas, not the sole contribution.
- Derive G-B only after fixing a clean latent-subgroup estimand and label channel.
- Demonstrate at least one multi-bin survival/RMST consequence not reducible to a scalar `pi*g` substitution.
- Complete a focused theorem-level literature comparison for robust subgroup, groupwise contamination, label misclassification, and censored subgroup analysis.
- Keep OT deferred and generation out of the primary theory.

## Alternatives rejected for now

- **R1:** Too strong; T1/T2 are not yet sufficient as a primary paper.
- **R2:** Too broad; general identification remains vulnerable to the Step 02 composition threat.
- **R4:** Premature; label contamination is under-specified and may become a subproblem of R3.
- **R5:** Not yet; restricted classes have not shown a meaningful threshold change.
- **R6:** Not warranted; T4/T6 remain plausible.
