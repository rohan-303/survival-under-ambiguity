# Pivot history

## Steps 01–02: contamination/censoring foundation

The project began with exact population identification under right censoring and Huber contamination. Step 02 derived the one-bin diameter and found the core result potentially reducible to Huber geometry composed with an observation operator. Novelty and sufficiency remained conditional.

## Step 03: rare-group fragility

Trusted-label rare-group formulas were derived, including the effective observable mass `pi_g g_g`. Label-contaminated models were separated because no universal conditional-survival formula exists without a frozen label channel. The project pivoted toward global versus groupwise audit information, but the strongest claims remained elementary or close to known robust-subgroup theory.

## Step 04: audit-information theory

Finite-grid LPs were implemented for global, marginal-preserving, group-capped, and known-label-channel contracts. The important negative result was that fixed-observed-law audit caps can be feasibility checks rather than width reductions. A joint RMST route was retained provisionally.

## Step 05: joint RMST geometry

The exact latent polytope, survival-vector map, support-function formulation, and RMST LP were implemented. The apparent RMST coupling gap was invalidated: Step 04 compared an unweighted sum of pointwise widths with an RMST width. With correct RMST weights, the intervals matched in the corrected scenario and the deterministic phase study. Gates J, M, N, and S therefore failed, and the joint-RMST branch is closed unless independently revived.

## Reusable infrastructure

- Exact Huber residual compatibility: `(1-epsilon) A p <= R`.
- Validated finite-grid censoring operators and strict survival convention `S(t)=P(T>t)`.
- LP support-function bounds for survival and RMST.
- Deterministic analytic-vs-LP verification scripts and evidence-labeled reports.
- Explicit literature-kill-test and gate documents.

## Step 06 pivot

The new provisional identity is **Auditing Survival Data Under Adversarial Corruption: Sharp Identification and Optimal Verification Design for Censored Records**. The primary question is whether gold-standard verification of the correct recorded tuple—not latent post-censoring event time—can produce a distinct minimax identification/audit-design theory. The finite-population adversarial model is selected for the initial preflight because its impossibility boundary can be tested exactly without importing estimator-efficiency assumptions.

No prior result was deleted or rewritten. The historical RMST error remains visible in the Step 05 correction.
