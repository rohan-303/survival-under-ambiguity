# M7 direction decision

## Selected path

**PATH C5 — IDEA IS INTERESTING BUT ALREADY OCCUPIED**

## Decision

The formal composition and invariance principle are coherent but elementary. The small paired pilot found a same-event/same-censor-count intervention pair with different censoring geometry and nonzero descriptive prediction discrepancy. However, SurvFM already reports paired censoring interventions holding latent prediction problems fixed and matched covariate-dependent-censoring diagnostics. Xu et al. already target administrative-cutoff shortcut leakage. The proposed core therefore does not clear the novelty or sufficiency gates.

This decision does not claim that every possible prediction-level falsification test is exhausted. It closes this formulation as a primary contribution. Do not begin Step 09 from this direction.

OT remains closed/deferred. Adversarial audit, exchangeable transfer, RMST geometry, and synthetic-generation branches remain closed or suspended under prior project policy.
