# Research Questions

All questions are **[OPEN]**.

1. **RQ1 [OPEN]:** What is the sharp identified set for selected survival functionals under censoring plus contamination in the simplest finite model?
2. **RQ2 [OPEN]:** How does recoverability scale with censoring support and contamination?
3. **RQ3 [OPEN]:** How does subgroup prevalence alter the worst-case result?
4. **RQ4 [OPEN]:** Which structural restrictions materially shrink the identified set?
5. **RQ5 [OPEN]:** Does robust-OT geometry yield tighter or more useful valid results than TV-native ambiguity?
6. **RQ6 [OPEN]:** How do finite-sample and estimated-censoring uncertainty change the result?
7. **RQ7 [OPEN]:** Can a finite collection of compatible synthetic worlds faithfully represent scientifically relevant ambiguity?
8. **RQ8 [OPEN]:** When can a decision be certified stable, and when should the method abstain?

## Candidate extensions

Potential latent classes include finite-grid laws, bounded hazards, monotone hazards, bounded-variation hazards, and smooth or piecewise-smooth hazards. These are candidates only; they are not implemented or endorsed by this bootstrap.
