# Survival under Ambiguity

Research foundation for **Survival under Ambiguity: Sharp Identification and Plausible Synthetic Worlds from Censored, Contaminated Data**.

## Status

**Foundation/Idea — Step 01 complete.** This repository contains the scientific contract and package smoke test only. No data, model, censoring operator, contamination estimator, theorem, or empirical result has been implemented.

## Research question

When right-censored survival observations may also be contaminated, which properties of the latent survival population remain identifiable, how sharply can they be bounded, and how should unresolved ambiguity be represented?

## Scope

The project will study partial identification, robust statistics, survival analysis, optimal-transport geometry, uncertainty quantification, and ambiguity-aware synthetic scenarios. Any novelty claim requires a literature and theorem-level audit first.
